Clinical Trials

Our clinical trials

Aftermind conducts clinical trials to evaluate intravenous DMT as a potential new treatment for acute ischemic stroke. Our Phase 1 study has been completed, and our Phase 2a study is preparing to launch in Hungary.

The study will enroll patients who have had an acute ischemic stroke caused by a large-vessel occlusion and who are treated at the participating stroke center. Recruitment details will be published here once the trial opens.
